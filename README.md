# JJAI Collection & Services — Official Website

**Slogan:** *Your All-In-One Solution!*  
**Core Values:** Quality • Creativity • Reliability  

This is the official responsive website for **JJAI Collection & Services**, a multi-service business based in Windhoek, Namibia. It replaces an earlier placeholder template and showcases all five core service divisions along with a direct WhatsApp quote/booking request system.

---

## 📋 Services Featured

1. **Professional Training**
   - Trading guidance (`ultravplsystem` platform & buying USD online for trading)
   - After-School Help (assignment assistance & academic tutoring)
2. **Graphic Design & Printing**
   - Fliers & posters for all events
   - Custom wedding cards & invitations
   - High-quality canvas prints (all sizes)
   - Booklet design (reports, brochures, manuals)
3. **Media & Photography**
   - Video & photo editing (content creation & post-production)
   - Studio & outdoor location photoshoots
   - Event coverage (weddings, graduations, corporate events)
4. **Logistics & Services**
   - Driving school (learn to drive & license preparation)
   - Import / ordering goods from South Africa & Angola
5. **Event & Wellness**
   - Catering services (events, buffets, custom menus)
   - Beauty services (hair, makeup, nails)
   - Cleaning services (post-event & residential house cleaning)

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Mobile-Responsive Layout:** Adapts cleanly to phones, tablets, and desktops without external frameworks.
- **Interactive Quote & Booking Form:** Allows visitors to select one or multiple services and send a pre-filled inquiry directly to WhatsApp.
- **Zero External Dependencies:** Built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript for fast loading and easy hosting.
